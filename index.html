Whitegate Observatory

Located in Whitegate, East Cork, Ireland, on the shores of Cork Harbour, Whitegate Observatory is a private amateur observatory dedicated to astronomy and radio science. The site benefits from relatively dark skies for an Irish coastal location, with the expanse of Cork Harbour providing excellent horizons for both optical and radio work.

The observatory runs automated forecasts and data collection around the clock, combining weather modelling and astronomical calculations to plan observing sessions and radio work.

What you'll find here

Radio

HF and VHF radio operations including amateur radio contacts and propagation monitoring.

Radio Astronomy

Passive radio astronomy observations including solar and Jupiter noise storm monitoring.

Astro Photography

Astrophotography outlook with a nightly scoring forecast for cloud cover, sky darkness, and seeing conditions.

Location

Whitegate, East Cork, Ireland — 51°49'N, 8°14'W. Situated on the eastern shore of Cork Harbour, one of the largest natural harbours in the world.
